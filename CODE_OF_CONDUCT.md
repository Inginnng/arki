# Community guidelines

Help people understand code and contribute with confidence. Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome questions and differing levels of experience; discuss technical ideas without personal attacks.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish someone else's personal information or private project contents.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. For a concern, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Inginnng); avoid sharing sensitive details publicly. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
